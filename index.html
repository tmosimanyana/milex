02 ABOUT MILEX FARM

A small farm with
big care.

Milex Farm is a working farm in Letlhakeng, Botswana. We grow vegetables in the field and raise chickens, goats, sheep and cattle, so the food we offer is food we know from start to finish.

We sell directly, with no shop and no middleman. You choose what you need, we confirm what is ready, and we get it to you, including delivery to Gaborone.

  • What we growLettuce, cabbage, rape, tomatoes, onions, beetroot, peppers, chillies and more, picked as the season allows.
  • What we raiseTswana chickens, broilers, goats, sheep and cattle, raised on our own land.
  • How you orderBuild an order on this page and send it to us on WhatsApp. We confirm the price with you directly.
